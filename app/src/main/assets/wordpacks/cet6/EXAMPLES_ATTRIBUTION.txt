The bundled bilingual examples were generated on 2026-09-16 from Tatoeba's textual
sentence exports. The package contains 3,178 sentence pairs covering 1,454 of the
2,219 CET6 core words; words without a direct bilingual match remain eligible for the
online fallback.

Source project: https://tatoeba.org/
English sentences: https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/eng/eng_sentences.tsv.bz2
Chinese sentences: https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/cmn/cmn_sentences.tsv.bz2
Direct links: https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/eng/eng-cmn_links.tsv.bz2

The textual Tatoeba corpus is released under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France
(CC BY 2.0 FR). Each row keeps its English/Chinese Tatoeba sentence identifiers in
source_sentence_id so the source can be attributed and audited. No audio files are
bundled. The app may fetch missing bilingual examples online when the device is
connected, while this package remains the offline fallback.
